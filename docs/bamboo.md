## 1. Programmes et boutons

### Description de l'appareil
The Bamboo Umai Induction Heating fuzzy logic rice cooker is a modern, multi-function cooking appliance built with high-quality manufacturing and years of expertise. It saves kitchen space by combining speciality rice cooking functions (white, short grain, brown, Yumami, GABA brown) with porridge, steam, slow cook, crust, and cake baking. The easy-to-use, state-of-the-art Korean Motouch control panel with adjustable timings simplifies cooking and produces delicious results.

- Soup ladle
- Measuring cup
- Steam basket

### Éléments principaux
- Lid
- Removable inner lid

- Gasket

- Outer body

### Panneau de commande
The control panel features touch-sensitive buttons:

- **START**: Press and hold for 2 seconds to activate the previously selected cooking program.
- **CANCEL**: Press to cancel a program and return to standby mode. Press and hold for 2 seconds to turn the unit on/off when in standby.
- **MENU**: Select from 10 automatic cooking programs (6 for rice). Each press cycles through the menu.
- **QUICK COOK**: For quick white rice cooking only.
- **KEEP WARM**: Press and hold for 2 seconds (when in standby) to activate Keep Warm mode. The button lights up, and a count-up clock appears.
- **PRESET**: Allows setting a timer for delayed cooking up to 24 hours. Be cautious with perishable foods.
- **HOURS / MINUTES**: Adjust cooking times for non-rice cooking menus and the preset function.

### Utiliser les commandes
The unit features a touch-sensitive 'cool ice white' Motouch panel. The 'START', 'KEEP WARM', and 'CANCEL' buttons require a 2-second press and hold to activate, indicated by the button lighting up and a fan starting.

To turn off the unit when no program is selected, press and hold the 'CANCEL' button for 2 seconds. To switch the unit on, press the 'CANCEL' button.

### Programmes de riz
- **'WHITE'**: For long grain white rice (e.g., basmati, jasmine, Thai fragrant).
- **'QUICK COOK'**: For white rice only, for faster cooking.
- **'BROWN'**: For long or short grain brown rice.
- **'SHORT GRAIN'**: For white short grain rice (e.g., pearl, sushi, Thai sticky rice). Refer to the table for water levels for sticky rice.
- **'GABA'**: Soaks and 'activates' brown rice to release gamma-aminobutyric acid (GABA), known for health benefits.
- **'YUMAMI'**: For any type of white rice. Enhances flavor by releasing sugars, making rice taste sweeter and 'moreish'.

### Autres programmes
PORRIDGE, STEAM, SLOW COOK, CRUST, CAKE (détails en sections 5 et 6).

### Maintien au chaud (KEEP WARM)
After cooking, the unit automatically switches to 'KEEP WARM'. You can activate it manually by pressing and holding 'KEEP WARM' for 2 seconds while in standby. The button lights up, and a count-up clock appears.

The 'Keep Warm' function lasts up to 24 hours, though rice may dry after 15 hours. You can extend freshness by adding a little water and stirring. To cancel 'KEEP WARM', press and hold 'CANCEL' for 2 seconds. If unplugged during Keep Warm, it will reactivate upon plugging back in; cancel it before selecting another setting.

### Annuler un programme
To cancel a program, keep warm, or stop mid-cycle, press and hold the 'CANCEL' button for 2 seconds. The unit will switch to standby mode.

### Départ différé (PRESET)
The 'PRESET' function allows you to set the appliance to have your rice or food ready at a specific time. It is available for all menu settings.

To set the timer:
1. Press 'MENU' to select a cooking program (for non-rice programs, you can set the cooking time here).
2. Press the 'PRESET' button; it will light up.
3. Use 'Hr' and 'Min' buttons to select the desired ready time.
4. Press and hold 'START'. The cooker will display the selected time, count down, and complete the cycle at the specified time.

If the preset time is set below the minimum stated, the cooking cycle will start immediately. This accounts for the default cooking time and fuzzy logic assessment time.

Suggested timings for the 'PRESET' function:

| Function | RICE | PORRIDGE | YUMAMI | STEAM | SLOW COOK | GABA |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Preset timings | 70 mins to 24 hrs | 70 mins to 24 hrs | 70 mins to 24 hrs | 1 hr to 24 hrs | 130 mins to 24 hrs | 150 mins to 24 hrs |

Note: Be aware that perishable foods should not be left for long periods when using the 'PRESET' function, as they may spoil.

The start time of the preset/timer is not exact; it may come on slightly earlier as Bamboo assesses the contents (rice/oats/water) and volume to calculate cooking time effectively.

## 4. Riz

Note: Maximum capacity is 8 cups (180ml) for WHITE rice and 6 cups (180ml) for BROWN rice.

Bamboo cooks rice using 7 distinct phases with IH Umai fuzzy logic for optimal nutrient retention and refined cooking, especially for GABA brown and Yumami settings.

During cooking, a chasing lines pattern appears on the display, with a countdown in the last 10 minutes.

### Première utilisation
1. Unpack the appliance and remove all packaging materials.
2. Remove accessories and the operating manual.
3. Remove any promotional stickers and wipe the appliance with a damp cloth.
4. Wash the inner bowl and lid in warm, soapy water with a soft sponge, then dry thoroughly.
5. Cook 0.5 cup of rice (with water filled to the 1/2 mark on the measuring cup) and discard it to prepare the cooker.

### Cuire le riz
1. Measure rice using the provided cup, leveling it at the top (180ml).
2. Clean rice if necessary. Place the inner bowl in the appliance, add rice, and fill to the corresponding water line for the amount/type of rice.
3. Close the lid and select the desired rice setting.
4. Press and hold 'START' to begin cooking.

Refer to the 'Speedy Start Guide' on the warranty card for a step-by-step guide and page 13 for more detailed tips.

For 1 cup of rice, use the measuring cup for water: 1 cup water for white rice, 1½ cups for brown rice.

### How to cook perfect rice
- **Measure accurately**: Use only the provided 180ml measuring cup and level the rice.
- **Rinse the rice**: Use a separate bowl or sieve. Stir loosely by hand and drain 2-3 times until the water runs clear. Avoid washing rice in the inner bowl to prevent scratches.
- **Be accurate with water**: The water amount significantly affects texture. For perfect rice, fill to the underside of the corresponding line on the inner bowl. For softer rice, fill to the top of the line; for harder rice, fill 2-3mm below the line.
- **Stir and loosen**: Once cooking is finished and the unit switches to 'KEEP WARM', stir and loosen the rice with the spatula to release excess moisture and achieve fluffy rice.

### Our tips for rice cooking
- Choose rice with few broken grains, as they make rice soggy and sticky.
- Rice batches vary in water absorption and age. Newer rice may need less water.
- If rice is too sticky, try rinsing less or not at all for certain varieties.
- If rice is too soft, reduce water by 3-4mm next time. If too hard, add 3-4mm more water.
- ❌ Do not use quick cook, partially cooked, or parboiled rice, as they are not suited for this type of rice cooker.

### GABA brown
The GABA brown rice function uses specific timing and temperatures to allow brown rice to germinate. It soaks and activates the brown rice using the Umai fuzzy logic control, then phases into the cooking process. During this, the inner bowl and contents are kept at a specific temperature to enable the rice to 'sprout'. The full cooking time, including activation, is approximately 2 hours 25 minutes. This process enhances flavor and increases nutrients like gamma-aminobutyric acid (GABA), resulting in a softer texture and nuttier flavor.

Nutritional Value Comparison:
- **WHITE RICE (DEAD)**: Nutritional Value: Carbohydrates. Components: Endosperm.
- **BROWN RICE (ASLEEP)**: Nutritional Value: Dietary Fibre, Vitamins, Minerals. Components: Endosperm, Bran, Cereal Germ.
- **GABA RICE (ALIVE)**: Nutritional Value: Rich in Gamma-Aminobutyric Acid (GABA), Band E Vitamins, Calcium, Potassium, Magnesium, and more. Components: Endosperm, Bran, Cereal Germ.

### Yumami
The 'Yumami' or 'extra tasty' rice function enhances the flavor of any white rice using a specially designed cooking cycle. It involves soaking and steaming to release sugars, which are then reabsorbed into the rice, altering their chemical structure and attaching to the rice surface. This process activates Umami receptors on the tongue, resulting in more flavorful rice with improved texture and taste. The rice is typically fluffier, softer, and sweeter. The full cooking time for Yumami is 1 hour and 6 minutes.

## 5. Vapeur (STEAM)

### Programme
Use the provided steaming basket for various foods. Default time is 10 minutes, adjustable up to 1 hour in 10-minute intervals. A countdown appears once water heats up. Using hot water speeds up heating. Fill to the 2-3 water level mark for stand-alone steaming. See Section 5, page 14 for steaming guide.

### Utilisation
This appliance can be used as a stand-alone steamer (using 1.5-2 cups of water) or to steam food while cooking rice. If steaming with rice, use the rice function for the rice and add the steaming basket above the rice in the inner bowl.

## 6. Slow cook et autres programmes

### SLOW COOK
For stews, curries, dhal, etc. Default time is 3 hours, adjustable from 2 to 8 hours. Heats to 97°C, then 87°C for slow cooking. This function is more efficient than conventional slow cookers, cooking recipes in 3-4 hours that normally take 7-8 hours.

Switches to 'KEEP WARM' upon completion.

Capacité : 4 litre slow cooker (spécifications) ; tableau des temps : 1.5 litres, 2 hours, can be adjusted to 8 hours maximum.

### PORRIDGE
Suitable for Asian rice porridge or oatmeal porridge. Default time is 1.5 hours, adjustable from 1 to 3 hours using 'Hr' or 'Min' buttons. Switches to 'KEEP WARM' upon completion.

### CRUST
For Persian style 'Tahdig' rice. Default time is 1.5 hours, adjustable from 1 to 2 hours. You can open the lid after a period to add ingredients (butter, oil, spices). Switches to 'KEEP WARM' upon completion.

### CAKE
For delicious, moist cakes. Default time is 40 minutes, adjustable from 30 to 60 minutes in 5-minute intervals. Switches to 'KEEP WARM' upon completion. See Section 6, page 15 for cake baking instructions.

How to bake cake:
1. Grease the inner bowl lightly and evenly with butter or oil.
2. Prepare the cake batter. Avoid using too much baking powder or soda, which can cause the cake to rise too high. The maximum weight for all ingredients is 600g.
3. Place the inner bowl on a flat surface, add the cake mixture, and gently tap the underside of the bowl several times to release air bubbles.
4. Place the inner bowl into the appliance and close the lid. Select the 'CAKE' setting. Adjust the cooking time from the default 40 minutes (30 to 60 minutes in 5-minute intervals). Press and hold 'START' for 2 seconds to begin.
5. Once cooking is finished, the appliance switches to 'KEEP WARM'. Test the cake with a toothpick. If not cooked, cancel 'KEEP WARM' (hold 'CANCEL' for 2 seconds) and re-select 'CAKE' to cook longer.

See page 17 for cake recipes.

NOTE: Do not place other cake pans or wax paper into the appliance or inner bowl, as this may cause malfunction or deform the bowl.

## 7. Céréales et légumineuses

This rice cooker can also prepare other grains and pulses. Here is a guide for cooking times/settings:

- **QUINOA**: Rinse well in cold water. Use a 1:1 ratio of quinoa to water (1:1.25 for black quinoa). Use the 'Quick Cook' setting. Fluff and keep warm for 5-10 minutes after cooking.
- **COUS COUS and GIANT COUS COUS**: Use water levels indicated on the packet and the 'Quick Cook' setting.
- **MILLET**: For each portion, measure ¼ cup millet into the inner bowl and add 1 cup water (e.g., 1 cup millet = 4 cups water). Add a pinch of salt. Select the 'Porridge' cycle. Adjust timing for desired consistency or use the preset timer. Add flavorings after cooking.
- **LENTILS and BEANS**: Use the 'Slow Cook' setting. Rinse beforehand and follow water levels on the pack. The 'Porridge' setting can also be used for lower temperatures.
- **PEARL BARLEY**: Use the 'Slow Cook' setting. Rinse before cooking and follow water levels on the pack. A large batch for soup will cook within an hour.

## 9. Nettoyage et entretien

Always remove food remnants and clean immediately after use.

- Do not use metal brushes, scrubbers, or harsh chemicals.
- Do not immerse the appliance in water. Use a damp sponge or soft cloth for the outer case.
- Do not wash rice in the inner bowl, as this can cause scratches and deteriorate the coating. Damage from washing rice in the bowl is not covered by warranty.
- **IMPORTANT: Never put the inner bowl or inner lid into a dishwasher.** The chemicals and washing cycle are too harsh. Damage from dishwasher use is not covered by warranty.

**Inner lid care** — The inner lid is removable. Push the lock release upwards to detach. Wash the lid with a sponge in soapy water after each use. To reattach, slide the bottom section behind the grooves and push the top until it clicks.

**Important inner bowl care** — Use only the provided plastic accessories, or wooden or silicone ones. Never use metal, as it will damage the ceramic coating. If food is stuck, pre-soak with a little washing-up liquid. Do not pour vinegar into the inner bowl, as it will damage the ceramic coating.

The outside (underneath) of the inner bowl may discolour and scuff due to heat; this is normal. The ceramic coating may also change color over time, which is normal.

**Steam cap care** — The steam cap on the lid is removable. Wash it regularly. It can be fully disassembled for thorough cleaning.

**Condensation collector care** — The condensation collector on the back is removable. Pull the small handle straight out. Remove, empty, wash, dry, and reinstall.

### Rice porridge (congee)
Ingredients (4-5 servings): 1 cup of rice; 75g chicken thighs, chopped and parboiled; Pinch of salt; Ginger and spring onion (scallion), shredded – to taste.

1. Wash rice, place in inner bowl, add water (follow inner bowl levels), then place chicken on top.
2. Press 'MENU', select 'PORRIDGE', and press and hold 'START'.
3. When the cooker switches to 'Keep Warm', add salt if desired and garnish with ginger and spring onions.

### Oatmeal porridge
Ingredients (serves 4 – do not exceed quantities): 1 cup steel cut or high-quality large rolled oats (sieve and shake hard 3-4 times to dislodge sediment); 3 cups water (measured in provided cup); 1 cup milk (or coconut milk or other non-dairy milk); 3-4 spoons (or to taste) honey or brown sugar. Experiment with oat and water amounts for desired thickness.

1. Place oats and water in the inner cooking pan.
2. Place the pan in the cooker, plug in, select 'PORRIDGE', and adjust timing (longer = thicker, shorter = thinner). Press and hold 'START'.
3. When it switches to keep warm, open the lid, stir, and add remaining ingredients. Soaking oats overnight using the Timer function softens texture.

Add dried or fresh fruit instead of sugar for a healthy option.
Do not use milk to cook oats; it can cause a 'boil over'. Only use water.

### Sushi rolls
Ingredients (4-5 servings): 3 cups of rice. For sushi seasoning (vinegar mix): 4 tbsp rice vinegar, 1 tbsp sugar, 1.5 tsp salt. Toppings: sashimi, prawns, pickles, natto, salmon roe, eel. Garnish: Seaweed, wasabi, soy sauce, pickled red ginger.

1. Wash rice, place in inner bowl, add water (follow levels), use 'SHORT GRAIN' option.
2. When it switches to 'Keep Warm', place rice in a clean, wet-cloth-wiped wooden container. Pour vinegar mix over rice, mixing while cooling with a fan.
3. Wrap your chosen ingredient with nori (seaweed).

### Rice pudding
Ingredients (cup measures are for the rice measuring cup, 4-6 servings): 2 cups arborio, short-grain, or sticky/glutinous rice; ½ cup evaporated milk; ½ cup coconut milk; 1 cup sweetened condensed milk; 1 cinnamon stick or pinch of ground cinnamon; 1 lemon zest, one large piece of lemon peel; ½ teaspoon nutmeg (or less if preferred); A sprinkle of ground cinnamon (to garnish).

1. Measure short grain rice with the provided cup.
2. Rinse rice in cold water, place rice and water (to correct line) in the cooker, select 'SHORT GRAIN'.
3. Whisk evaporated milk, coconut milk, condensed milk, cinnamon, lemon zest, and nutmeg together.
4. When rice is cooked and on 'Keep Warm', add the whisked milks to the rice, close lid. Check consistency after 30 minutes.
5. Discard cinnamon stick (if used) and lemon zest.
6. Serve in individual dishes, garnish with ground cinnamon and cream if desired.

** Vegan adaptation: Omit evaporated and condensed milk. Use one whole tin of coconut milk and some non-dairy milk to loosen if needed after stage 5.

### Cakes
For cakes, use a ready-made mix or follow the guidance on maximum ingredients for homemade batter.

**Banana cake** — Ingredients: 3 eggs; 110g sugar; 110g melted butter; 200g very ripe bananas (weight without skin); 160g plain flour; 3/4 tsp baking powder; 3/4 tsp baking soda; A pinch of salt, less than 1/8 tsp; Butter for greasing the inner bowl.

1. Sift flour, baking powder, soda, and salt. Melt butter and sugar, let cool. Mash bananas.
2. Beat eggs into butter/sugar mixture.
3. Add mashed bananas and mix.
4. Fold in sifted flour mixture.
5. Pour batter into greased bowl. Tap the bottom of the bowl several times to remove air bubbles.
6. Select 'CAKE' and set time to at least 50 minutes. Test with a toothpick after the cycle. If not firm or mixture sticks, cook longer.
7. Remove bowl and let cake cool. Invert onto a plate.
8. Serve with whipped cream, ice cream, caramel sauce, or enjoy as is.

**Fudge cheesecake** — Ingredients: 200g Cream cheese; 50g sugar; 30g sugar (for beating egg whites); 3 eggs (separate yolk from white); 30g all-purpose flour – sifted; 30ml fresh cream; 4 tsp lemon juice; Vanilla extract to taste; 20g butter, melted (for greasing inner bowl).

1. Grease the inner bowl. Soften cream cheese (microwave for 30 seconds if needed).
2. Mix cream cheese until smooth, add sugar.
3. Add yolks one at a time. Blend in flour, cream, lemon juice, vanilla, and melted butter. Add fudge pieces.
4. In a separate bowl, beat egg whites until foamy. Gradually add sugar to form meringue.
5. Gently fold meringue into cream cheese mixture.
6. Pour batter into the inner bowl. Tap the bottom several times to release air bubbles. Place bowl in cooker and close lid.
7. Press 'MENU', select 'CAKE', set cooking time to 60 minutes, and press 'START'.
8. When baking is complete, allow to cool, then carefully invert onto a pastry case or biscuit base.
